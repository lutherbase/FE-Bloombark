👋 Getting started

Bloombark Terminal is a token intelligence platform for the Robinhood chain — risk analysis, wallet tracking, trading, and community, all in one place. Most read-only features (AI Token Analyzer, Market Overview, Narrative) work without connecting a wallet.

Click Connect Wallet (top right) to unlock Trade, Watchlist, Alerts, and Community — MetaMask is currently the supported wallet.

ANALYSIS

AI Token Analyzer

Paste any Robinhood token contract address to get a full risk breakdown: holder concentration, honeypot and mint-function checks, liquidity health, insider wallet detection, and a live price chart.

The Bloombark Risk Score (0–100) is calculated from real on-chain data — higher means riskier. Use it as a starting point, not a guarantee.

AI Prediction

A rule-based scoring engine — not a black-box model — that weighs price momentum, buy/sell pressure, volume trend, liquidity, holder distribution, and security flags into a BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL call with a confidence score.

Available from the AI Token Analyzer page after scanning a token. This is not financial advice — it's a second opinion built from the same data you can see yourself in the signal breakdown.

AI Track Record

Every BULLISH or BEARISH AI Prediction call is logged, then checked ~24 hours later against the real price move. No cherry-picking — wins and losses are both shown, with a running win rate.

NEUTRAL calls aren't logged, since there's no clear right answer to resolve them against.

TRADING

Trade

Swap any Robinhood token directly from the terminal, routed via KyberSwap for best pricing. Supports both market orders (instant, at current price) and limit orders (gasless, executes when your target price hits).

Every swap requires MetaMask confirmation — Bloombark never holds your funds or executes trades on your behalf.

Market Overview

A live dashboard across supported chains — Pools, Trending, Top Gainers, and New Pools tabs, plus chain-wide volume and transaction stats.

This is price/volume-based trending sourced from GeckoTerminal — different from Trending on Bloombark, which tracks activity on the terminal itself.

Wallet Tracker

Paste any EVM wallet address to see its full token holdings, portfolio value, and transaction history across chains.

Useful for checking a token's top holders or creator wallet before you trade — see exactly what else they hold.

Watchlist & Comparison

Save tokens from AI Token Analyzer with the ♡ icon to track them in one table — price, volume, mcap, liquidity, and age.

Click Compare Token to pick 2–5 watchlist tokens and see them ranked side by side on an overall score built from liquidity, volume, momentum, holders, and risk.

Alerts

Set Market Cap, Volume, or Price alerts on any watchlist token. Price alerts fire every time the live price touches your target, in either direction; Market Cap/Volume alerts fire on a % move from baseline. Enable browser push notifications to get notified even with the tab closed.

SOCIAL

Narrative

Tracks emerging market themes and sector rotations, so you can spot which narratives are gaining traction before they're obvious.

Community

Live chat rooms — General, Trading, Alpha, Free Shill, plus gated Holders and Private rooms. Paste a contract address in chat and the built-in bot replies with an instant AI Prediction card.

$BBRK Moon is a read-only feed that auto-posts every on-chain buy of the $BBRK token.

Trending on Bloombark

Shows what's actually happening on the terminal itself, refreshed every 24 hours: the most-scanned tokens in AI Token Analyzer, most-discussed in Community (excluding Holders, Private, and $BBRK Moon), and most-traded on Trade. Each row shows the token's real logo and links straight into AI Token Analyzer.

UTILITY

Accessibility

Toggle Colorblind mode in the sidebar footer to swap the green/red up-down convention for a blue/orange pair that stays distinguishable across common color-vision deficiencies — applies everywhere, including charts.

DATA SOURCES

Data Sources

Bloombark doesn't run its own indexer — every number you see is pulled live from these public APIs and on-chain RPCs. No data is fabricated or estimated.

DexScreener

api.dexscreener.com/latest/dex/tokens/{address}

Price, volume, liquidity, price chart, and market cap/FDV — used across AI Token Analyzer, Watchlist, Comparison, Trade, and Alerts (including Price alert checks).

Blockscout

{chain}.blockscout.com/api/v2/addresses/{address}

Wallet token balances, native balances, and transaction history — powers Wallet Tracker and Chain Transactions, per-chain (Robinhood, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon).

GeckoTerminal

api.geckoterminal.com/api/v2/networks/{network}/pools

Pools, Trending, Top Gainers, and New Pools tabs in Market Overview — price/volume-based trending, separate from Trending on Bloombark's activity data.

GoPlus Security

api.gopluslabs.io/api/v1/token_security/{chainId}

Honeypot detection, mint-function checks, holder concentration, and other risk flags — feeds the Bloombark Risk Score and Wallet Relationship Map.

KyberSwap

aggregator-api.kyberswap.com · limit-order.kyberswap.com

Swap routing and quotes for Trade — both market orders (aggregator) and gasless limit orders.

CoinGecko

api.coingecko.com/api/v3/coins/categories

Sector/category market data — powers Narrative's theme and rotation tracking.

DefiLlama

api.llama.fi/overview/dexs/{chain}

Chain-wide DEX volume overview — supplements Market Overview's chain stats.

On-chain RPC

*-rpc.publicnode.com · api.mainnet-beta.solana.com

Direct JSON-RPC calls (EVM chains + Solana) for on-chain scanning and enrichment jobs that public APIs don't cover.

FAQ

Is Bloombark custodial?

No. Bloombark never holds your funds or private keys. Every trade is signed and confirmed in your own MetaMask wallet.

Is the AI Prediction financial advice?

No. It's a rule-based signal built from public on-chain data, shown for research purposes — always do your own due diligence.

Which chain does Bloombark support?

Robinhood chain is the primary focus for Trade, Alerts, and Sniper-style features. Wallet Tracker and AI Token Analyzer also support Ethereum, Base, and Arbitrum for read-only lookups.
